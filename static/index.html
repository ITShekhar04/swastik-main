1. WHAT IS THE DOPPLER EFFECT?
Change in frequency due to relative motion. In radar, signal travels to target and reflects back:
f_D = (2 * v_r) / λ = (2 * v_r * f_0) / c
Factor 2 accounts for two-way radar round trip.
2. HOW DOPPLER HELPS SCANNING
  • Detects Moving Objects: Moving targets produce measurable shift.
  • Estimates Velocity: Amount of shift directly gives radial speed.
  • Micro-Doppler Analysis: Rotating blades create harmonic modulation.
